### Hi, I'm Tehreem

I'm an electrical engineer from Karachi (BS Electrical Engineering, electronics stream, FAST-NUCES, 2023). I work as an instructor in the Department of Computer Science at FAST-NUCES, where I run the labs for Operating Systems, Digital Logic Design, ICT, Programming for Business, Databases and IT in Business.

My final-year project detected fire on a Raspberry Pi with a TensorFlow Lite model and turned a camera toward it with a servo. My recent projects are open simulation studies in power and energy: a home PV and battery system under scheduled load-shedding in Karachi, thermal wear of electric scooter motor controllers in hot cities, and the effect of e-scooter home charging on a Lahore neighbourhood transformer. Each repository has the code, data and results, so every number can be reproduced.

**Pinned work**
- [pv-battery-ems-karachi](https://github.com/tehreem-gillani/pv-battery-ems-karachi): how a home battery should hold backup when load-shedding is scheduled; a look-ahead reserve rule serves 99.7 % of outage energy with 23 % less grid import than a tuned fixed reserve, and stays within 1.5-3.1 % of the perfect-foresight optimum
- [e2w-controller-wearout](https://github.com/tehreem-gillani/e2w-controller-wearout): thermal cycling of e-scooter motor controllers in Karachi, Delhi, Jakarta, Lagos and Madrid; hot cities add 1.1 to 2.6 times the damage of Madrid, while cooling and MOSFET count change it far more ([IEEE-format report](https://github.com/tehreem-gillani/e2w-controller-wearout/blob/main/report/Gillani_E2W_Controller_Climate.pdf))
- [lahore-heat-feeder](https://github.com/tehreem-gillani/lahore-heat-feeder): measured data from Lahore homes show the summer neighbourhood load peaking around midnight; on that feeder, e-scooter charging timed after the tariff peak ages the transformer 36 % faster, while charging that ends at departure adds 8 % ([IEEE-format report](https://github.com/tehreem-gillani/lahore-heat-feeder/blob/main/report/Gillani_Lahore_Heat_Feeder.pdf))

**Earlier work**
- [pv-mppt-shading](https://github.com/tehreem-gillani/pv-mppt-shading): MPPT under partial shading; event-triggered scan recovers 99.4 % of available energy vs 84.6 % for plain P&O
- [mppt-bit-width](https://github.com/tehreem-gillani/mppt-bit-width): how ADC resolution limits MPPT, and why low light sets the requirement
